Privacy Policy

Boulton Games. Effective October 29, 2026.

This page covers the Boulton Games website and the general approach for all Boulton Games apps. Each game also has its own privacy policy that describes exactly what that game does.

This website

This site has no ads, analytics, cookies or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic visit information such as IP addresses under GitHub's own privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log the request under Google's privacy policy.

Our games

Boulton Games apps are built to work offline and do not collect, upload, share or sell personal information. There are no accounts and no ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. Boulton Games never sees your payment details.
